About

Namaste! I am Kshitij, a software engineer specialising in web technologies. From front end to back end development I enjoy each aspect of building applications for the web.

My first introduction to web technology was in high school where I got learned HTML and CSS. I was in awe of this technology where I could bring my ideas to life from scratch and so easily share it with other people. Hence when I did my bachelors in engineering I majored in software engineering.

I am a big Javascript enthusiast. I started learning web frameworks with PHP back in 2014 where I would make small static web applications linked to a MariaDB SQL storage using the LAMP stack which did not have much interactivity so I started learning Javascript and fell in love with it. After graduating I worked with 2 small startups in Mumbai where I gained most of my skills working as a full stack developer. I moved to Australia to do a masters where I completed Masters of IT majoring in software engineering.

Outside my career I have a deep interest in psychoacoustics and have been learning sound design using a simple modular Eurorack system. I very much like physics and one of the reasons my interest is sounds is that it lets me indulge in that interest and understand the nature of our reality through sound exploration.
